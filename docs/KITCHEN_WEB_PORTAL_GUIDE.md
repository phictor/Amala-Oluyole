## Daily kitchen workflow

1. Open the portal before service and sign in.
2. In **Orders**, check the top-right operational status before service. It shows **Connected**, **Reconnecting**, or **Offline**, whether sound is enabled, and the time of the last successful update. Do not use preparation controls while the portal is Offline or Reconnecting.
3. Select **Enable sound** once at the beginning of service. The browser requires this single interaction before it can play a sound for new or changed orders.
4. Keep the cook’s preparation board in this sequence: **Awaiting branch acceptance → To prepare → Preparing → Ready for packing**. A paid order awaiting acceptance is deliberately separate from the cook’s board so it is not prepared prematurely.
5. Read every pack before preparation. Each ticket now shows the quantity, saved meal choices for custom meals—including swallow, soup, protein, and extras—plus visible kitchen instructions. It also shows the receipt time, preparation start time, target ready time, and an overdue warning where the target is exceeded.
6. Use the large action button once per ticket. Repeated taps are safe: the server records only one valid status change. A ticket can only move through the approved preparation sequence.
7. If an order was marked ready by mistake, select **Recall to preparation**, enter the reason, and save. The correction and reason are retained in the order history. Do not use Recall after rider handover or collection.
8. Open **Ingredients & purchases** to add every item used in food preparation. For protein, choose a prepared option such as **Cow Meat / Beef**, **Goat Meat**, **Chicken**, **Turkey**, **Croaker Fish**, **Tilapia**, **Titus Fish**, **Butterfish / Owere**, **Ponmo**, **Bokoto**, or **Assorted Meat**. Choose **Other protein** to type a different protein name.
9. When supplies arrive, use **Record a purchase**. Select the ingredient, enter the usable stock received and the **total amount paid**. The portal calculates the purchase cost per stock unit while the form is being completed, and shows the estimated new average cost before the record is saved.
10. For a whole animal or grouped item, record both its source and its yield. For example: select *Beef*; enter `1` as bought quantity; choose *whole cow*; add the number of pieces/quarters; enter the usable kilograms received after cutting; then enter the total purchase cost. This keeps the original purchase and the kitchen's usable stock clear.
11. Record **Kitchen use**, **Wastage**, or a verified **Stock adjustment** whenever stock leaves the store. Every movement records the signed-in staff member automatically.
12. Use **Edit** beside an ingredient or a saved purchase to correct any value. Saving an ingredient stock change adds an attributable adjustment; saving a purchase correction updates the connected stock balance and ingredient cost. The purchase can also be reassigned to the correct saved ingredient where a wrong ingredient was selected.
13. Use **Monthly report** to review monthly orders, daily production, ingredient purchases, current stock value, top meals, and low-stock items. The report refreshes after each saved purchasing change.

## If something does not look right

| Situation | Kitchen action |
|---|---|
| A new order has no dish list | Refresh the Orders page once. If it remains empty, do not prepare it; contact the manager with the order number. |
| The portal says Offline or Reconnecting | Do not press preparation, ready, or recall controls. Keep the page open; it reconnects and retrieves the authoritative queue automatically. If the status remains Offline, follow the restaurant’s interruption procedure and contact the Manager. |
| A ticket is overdue | Check the visible instruction and preparation target, then report the delay to the Manager before promising a collection time. |
| An order is marked ready in error | Use **Recall to preparation**, enter the reason, and confirm the ticket returns to Preparing. This is for a kitchen correction only; it does not undo a pickup verification or rider delivery event. |
| A meal is out of stock | Tell the manager to mark the meal unavailable from the Menu page immediately. |
| Ingredient details or opening stock were entered incorrectly | Select **Edit** next to the ingredient, correct the field, and save. A changed stock figure is recorded as an adjustment with your staff account. |
| A market purchase has not been recorded | Use **Record a purchase** immediately. Enter the actual amount paid and usable stock received; do not use a stock adjustment for a real purchase. |
| A saved purchase has an error | Select **Edit** next to that purchase, correct the ingredient, quantities, cost, supplier, receipt, date, or note, then save. The stock and cost summary update together. |
| An animal has been cut into portions | Keep the original purchase detail in **Bought quantity**, **Bought as**, and **Number of pieces**. Record only the usable quantity received into stock. |
| No sound is heard | Select **Enable sound** again and ensure the computer/tab is not muted. The visual banner and new-order column still update. |
| Staff cannot sign in | Confirm that the Manager has assigned the person the **Kitchen** role, that the work email is exact, and that they are using the current private kitchen access code. |
| “This email is not approved” | The Manager must create or update the staff record and assign the Kitchen role before the person can enter. |
| “Too many failed sign-in attempts” | Wait 15 minutes before trying again. Do not keep guessing the access code. |

## Important menu rule

For Amala meals, **Gbegiri is included by default**. **Ewedu is optional** and selected only for customers who prefer it. Neither accompaniment is sold as a standalone soup.

## Manager actions

The manager updates prices and availability from the **Menu** screen in the Admin / Manager portal. Mark an item unavailable immediately when it runs out; it disappears from customer ordering until reactivated.
