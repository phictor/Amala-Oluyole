## Staff procedure

Open **Admin → Menu Management**, find the meal, and select **Update photo** on its image. Choose the restaurant photograph from the device and wait for the confirmation. The new image appears in the customer menu as soon as it is saved.

For a meal that is not yet listed, select **Add Meal**, choose the appropriate menu section, enter the final selling price and description, save it, then return to the card and select **Update photo**. Never add a dish with an estimated price; use the current restaurant price list.

## Naming standard

Use **Amala & Ewedu + Gbegiri** for the standard three-part Ibadan serving. The term **Abula** is used where the serving includes amala alongside ewedu, gbegiri, and pepper sauce. This aligns the app with familiar Yoruba and Nigerian menu terminology. [1]

Use **Vegetable Soup (Efo Riro)** rather than a vague “vegetable soup” where the dish is the Yoruba-style leafy soup. Efo riro may be served with a swallow or rice. [2]

Use **Peppered Snail** only for snails cooked in a pepper sauce; list it as a protein or chef special based on the restaurant’s final menu and pricing. [3]
