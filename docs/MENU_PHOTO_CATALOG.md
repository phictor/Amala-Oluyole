## Staff procedure

In **Admin → Menu**, select a meal and choose **Update photo**. Before selecting a new photograph, confirm that the kitchen’s product name, portion, and price match the item on the card. For a dish not yet listed, add it with an approved price first, then attach the photograph. Never reuse a photo for a different dish simply to fill an empty card.

## Naming standard

Use **Gbegiri Soup** for the smooth bean soup. **Abula** describes a combined serving of amala, ewedu, gbegiri, and stew; it should not be used for a photograph of gbegiri alone. [1] Use **Efo Riro** for the Yoruba-style leafy vegetable stew and **Egusi Soup** for the melon-seed soup. [2]
